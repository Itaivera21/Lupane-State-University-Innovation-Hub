Quick Summary: This platform is for LSU students, faculty, and staff to collaborate on projects, share ideas, and build portfolios. You own your content, but you grant us permission to display it. Be respectful, don't steal others' work, and follow LSU policies. Your data is protected and not sold to third parties.

To use the Innovation Hub platform, you must:

  • Admin Accounts: For platform administrators to manage users, projects, and system settings.
  • Developer Accounts: For core developers to manage system operations and maintenance.

3.3 Account Termination — We reserve the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates these Terms, engages in harmful behavior, or remains inactive for an extended period.

5.3 Respect for Others' Work — You agree not to copy, reproduce, or claim ownership of other users' project ideas or content without their explicit permission. Violations may result in account termination.

Part 2: Privacy Policy

13. Information We Collect

When you register for the Innovation Hub, we collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, student ID, faculty, department
  • Account Information: Username, password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)
  • User Content: Projects, messages, forum posts, uploaded files
  • Usage Data: Login times, pages visited, features used

14. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve platform services
  • To facilitate project collaboration and communication
  • To display project portfolios (with your consent)
  • To communicate important updates and notifications
  • To ensure platform security and prevent abuse

15. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information:

  • With your project team members (name, skills, contact)
  • With project supervisors (when assigned to your project)
  • With industry partners (portfolio projects only, with appropriate consent)
  • When required by law or to protect rights and safety

16. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Password hashing (bcrypt) — never stored in plain text
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Database access controls and regular security reviews
  • Secure cloud hosting with TiDB Cloud

17. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Control visibility of your projects (private vs public)
  • Opt out of promotional communications

18. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active. Account deletion will remove your personal data, but some anonymized data may be retained for analytical purposes. Project data may be retained for academic and research purposes.

19. Children's Privacy

The Innovation Hub is intended for university students and faculty. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

20. International Data Transfer

Your information may be stored on servers located outside Zimbabwe (TiDB Cloud infrastructure). By using the platform, you consent to such transfer, understanding that data protection laws may differ from those in Zimbabwe.

21. Cookies and Tracking

We use session cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. These are essential for platform functionality. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising.

22. Third-Party Services

The platform may use third-party services such as:

  • Render: Web hosting and deployment
  • TiDB Cloud: Database hosting
  • GitHub: Code repository and version control

These services have their own privacy policies which we recommend you review.

23. Changes to Privacy Policy

We will notify users of any material changes to this Privacy Policy via email or platform notification. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest changes.

If you have any questions about these Terms of Service or Privacy Policy, please contact us:
